Status: draft awaiting ticket-breakdown review; proposed label `ready-for-agent`.

## What to build

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Equal, unequal, exact, percentage and shares methods retain shared precision and conservation behavior, including multiple payers.
- [ ] Show the remaining amount or percentage when meaningful; do not silently round or redistribute invalid entries.
- [ ] Done and Android Back retain draft entries; correction preserves other fields and does not turn an unresolved attempt into new data.
- [ ] Verify a two-person create/edit correction journey plus controller tests for each invalid allocation class.
