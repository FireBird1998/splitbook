## What to build

To review on Android, part 1: finding your items and saving the ready ones. It replaces the hidden "Your private Drafts" modal and Home's "Continue" rows.

- **Home card.** "To review · 5" sits above Your balances.
  - One row per Group, showing the name, source summary ("2 from Claude · 1 needs details") and a count badge.
  - A row for each item kept only on this phone ("Groceries · ₹1,250.00 · Unfinished · only on this phone · Maple House", plus **Continue**, which opens the form as today).
  - **Review N, one at a time**, which ticket 6 makes work. Until then it opens the first Group's list.
  - "Only you see these".
  - Hidden when nothing is waiting.
  - Offline, it shows "You're offline. What Claude prepared and what you set aside show up when you're back online", and still lists the phone-only items.
- **Group banner.** At the top of a Group's Expenses when that Group has items: "3 to review · 2 from Claude today · 1 needs details" › Review.
- **To review list for a Group.**
  - The privacy line, then a summary with ready, needs details and on this phone, followed by **Save N ready** and **Review one at a time**.
  - Sections: Ready, Check before saving, Needs details, On this phone. Each row shows its source, amount, date and what's missing.
  - The empty state is "All caught up".
- **Save N ready.**
  - A confirmation sheet lists each item, the exact total, and "Balances there will change for everyone".
  - Confirming saves exactly those versions.
  - A results sheet shows each item as saved, failed, or "We didn't hear back, so it may already be saved", with **Check again** (original identity, never twice).
- **Links.** Android opens `/groups/<id>/review` links (from Claude's replies) on that Group's To review list, through the existing app links.
- **What goes.** The "Your private Drafts" button and modal and Home's "Continue" rows are removed. Any item they held now appears in To review: server Drafts as themselves, and phone-only items under "On this phone".

Design: the ReviewHome, ReviewGroup and ReviewList boards, all states (waiting, offline, all caught up, confirm Save 2 ready, after saving).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The Home card, Group banner and list use the cross-Group read and the shared To review model. Their counts, sections and source summaries match the model.
- [ ] Phone-only items (existing form recovery) appear on Home and in their Group's list as "On this phone", and Continue opens the form.
- [ ] The Save N ready confirmation lists exactly the ready items and their exact total. Confirming saves exactly those versions, and the results show each item's outcome, including Check again. A lost-reply test proves Check again never saves twice.
- [ ] Offline: the card shows the offline note and the phone-only items, and nothing tries to load or save account items.
- [ ] `/groups/<id>/review` app links open the list.
- [ ] The old private Drafts modal and Home "Continue" rows are gone, and nothing they held is lost.
- [ ] Render tests through the mobile controller cover every state above, at 130% text and in dark. The mobile HTTP verifier covers the cross-Group read and Save N ready.
