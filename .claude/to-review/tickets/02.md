## What to build

Members see on the web that something is waiting for them: a To review card on Home, and a banner inside each Group. Underneath are the two pieces both clients will share.

- **A pure To review model in `@splitbook/shared`.** It takes a member's Drafts (with their completeness, possible-duplicate result and who prepared them) plus any items kept only on a device. It works out:
  - each item's state: **ready**, **possible duplicate**, **needs details** (naming the missing facts, for example "who paid"), or **on this phone**;
  - the run order: ready, then possible duplicate, then needs details, then on this phone, oldest first within each;
  - counts per Group and in total;
  - a short source summary ("2 from Claude · 1 needs details");
  - the "Save N ready" selection with its exact total, per currency, never mixing currencies;
  - an end-of-run summary from per-item outcomes ("Saved 2 · Deleted 1 · 2 left for later").

  It reuses the existing incomplete and complete Expense validation and the existing duplicate rule. It doesn't invent its own.
- **A members-only cross-Group To review read.** It returns the member's active Drafts across all their current Groups, grouped by Group. Each Draft carries who prepared it and its current possible-duplicate result (the same rule as the existing review step), so a client can tell "ready" from "check before saving" without another request.
  - It follows the shared path, query key and decoder pattern of the other reads.
  - Groups the member has left are left out.
  - The existing per-Group Draft list stays as it is.
- **Web Home card.** It sits above Your balances, reads "To review · 5", with one row per Group (name, source summary, count) and "Only you see these". Each row links to that Group's To review page (`/groups/<id>/review`, built in ticket 3). It is hidden entirely when nothing is waiting, and has loading and error states like the other Home cards.
- **Web Group banner.** It appears at the top of a Group's Expenses tab when that Group has items: "3 to review · 2 from Claude today · 1 needs details", plus Review. It is hidden when there are none.

Design: the ReviewHome and ReviewGroup boards (phone), adapted to the web Home and Group layouts.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The shared model's unit tests cover every state, including several missing facts at once, the run order, counts, the source summary, the ready selection, totals with two currencies kept apart, and the end-of-run summary. They pass in all four CI time zones.
- [ ] The cross-Group read returns only the member's own active Drafts in Groups they currently belong to. A Group they left, someone else's Drafts, and saved or discarded Drafts never appear.
- [ ] It refuses the same way the other reads do: 401 when signed out, and nothing at all when the flag is off. Existing reads are unchanged.
- [ ] Each item carries who prepared it and its current possible-duplicate result.
- [ ] Integration tests cover the read: membership, a Group left and a Group rejoined, other members' Drafts, and a possible duplicate against a saved Expense.
- [ ] The web Home card and Group banner show the right counts and source summary, link to the Group's To review page, and disappear when empty. Each has loading and error states.
- [ ] Playwright on the demo seed with Drafts prepared by a fake assistant: the card, the banner, and their links. Axe passes in light and dark.
