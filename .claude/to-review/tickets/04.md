## What to build

Two things the whole Group or the web form needs.

**1. "Prepared by" on saved Expenses**
- An Expense saved from a Draft that a Connected assistant prepared records that, with the assistant's display name.
- The member who saved it stays its creator.
- Expenses from the member's own Drafts, or from the form, record nothing extra.
- On the web it shows:
  - in the Group's Expense list ("Sam paid · prepared by ChatGPT");
  - in the Expense side panel;
  - in Activity ("Alex added Electricity · prepared by Claude").
- It is additive to every existing Expense and Activity read, so Android and the mobile HTTP verifiers keep working. Android displays it in ticket 7.

**2. Finish later in the web Add expense form**
- **The button.** "Keep as Draft" becomes **Finish later**, with the help line "Keeps it in To review. Only you can see it." It creates a Draft prepared by the member and closes the form, with a confirmation ("Kept in To review", plus View).
- **Closing.** Closing the Add expense form after typing or choosing anything asks **Finish later** · **Delete** · **Keep editing**. Closing an empty form asks nothing.
- **At the limit.** Finish later is disabled with "You have 100 items waiting in this Group. Save or delete some before keeping more." and a link to To review.
- **If Finish later fails** (network or server), the form stays open with its content and says so. Nothing is lost.
- **Scope.** Editing an existing Expense is unchanged.

Design: the ReviewForm board (phone; its closing, full and kept states) and the ReviewGroup board's "prepared by ChatGPT" row.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Saving an assistant-prepared Draft records who prepared it on the Expense. Saving the member's own Draft, or using the form, records nothing extra.
- [ ] Integration tests prove the Expense and Activity reads add the field without changing anything existing. The mobile HTTP verifiers still pass.
- [ ] The web shows "prepared by <assistant>" in the Expense list, the side panel and Activity.
- [ ] Finish later creates a Draft prepared by the member and confirms it. It is disabled at the limit with the per-member text.
- [ ] The close prompt appears only when the form has changes, and each of its three choices does what it says.
- [ ] A failed Finish later keeps the form open with its content.
- [ ] Playwright covers: Finish later, the close prompt with each choice, the limit state, and "prepared by" in the list and Activity. Axe passes in light and dark.
