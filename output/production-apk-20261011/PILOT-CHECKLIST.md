# Production Android pilot

Install SplitBook-Production.apk on your phone first. It uses the live splitbook.in ledger.

Before the friend signs in, add their exact Google email to both the Google OAuth test-user list and the production AUTH_ALLOWED_EMAILS allowlist.

With one friend: sign in, create a small Group, share an invitation and join, add an Expense, verify the same payer/split/balances on both phones, edit the Expense, record a payment and verify both balances. Test restart, a saved draft, offline reads, reconnect and sign-out; financial saves must require connection and explicit Save. Check invitation opening from a messaging app.

Do not expand to the remaining friends until this pilot passes. Back up the production signing key and password securely. Physical-phone acceptance remains open under #193.
